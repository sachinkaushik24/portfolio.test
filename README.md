## 🔒 Private Owner Access: How to Edit Your Portfolio

To keep your website formal and clean, all editing capabilities are **completely hidden from visitors**.

### How to Unlock the Editor:
You have 3 private ways to open the editor:
1. **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd> anywhere on the website.
2. **Logo Shortcut**: Rapidly click/tap the **"SK"** logo 3 times in the top navigation bar.
3. **Secret URL**: Open your website with `?admin=true` (e.g. `index.html?admin=true`).

### Security PIN:
- When the verification prompt appears, enter your Security PIN: **`2404`**
- Once verified, the **Portfolio Customizer** opens, and an **Owner Mode** bar appears at the bottom right.
- You can change your PIN, text, and card background image URLs anytime in the editor or in `profile-data.js`.

### Applying & Saving Changes:
- **"Apply Live Changes"**: Updates the site in your browser immediately.
- **"Download profile-data.js"**: Downloads your updated data file with one click. Simply replace the `profile-data.js` file in your project folder to persist updates permanently!
- Click **"Lock"** in the floating Owner Mode bar to exit and lock owner mode.
